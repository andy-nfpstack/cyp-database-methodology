Domains and indicators

Economic hardship

  • Children in relative low income families (%) (Financial year ending March 2025)
  • Pupils eligible for free school meals (January 2025)
  • Income deprivation affecting children: average score (IoD 2025)

Family risk

  • Lone-parent households (Census Day, 21 March 2021)
  • Domestic abuse identified at assessment (1 April 2024 to 31 March 2025): practice-sensitive
  • Parental mental health identified at assessment (1 April 2024 to 31 March 2025): practice-sensitive
  • Parental alcohol misuse identified at assessment (1 April 2024 to 31 March 2025): practice-sensitive
  • Parental drug misuse identified at assessment (1 April 2024 to 31 March 2025): practice-sensitive

Health

  • Hospital admissions for self-harm (10-24) (Latest year ending on or before 31 March 2025)
  • Hospital admissions for injuries (0-14) (Latest year ending on or before 31 March 2025)
  • Disabled children and young people (Census Day, 21 March 2021)

Education

  • Persistent absence from school (Academic year 2024/25)
  • Suspensions from school (Academic year 2024/25 (check))
  • 16-17 year olds NEET or activity not known (End 2024 (Dec 2024 - Feb 2025 average))

Housing

  • Children in temporary accommodation (At 31 March 2025)
  • Overcrowded households with children (Census Day, 21 March 2021)

Safety

  • First-time entrants to youth justice (Year ending 31 December 2024)
  • Violence and sexual offences (1 April 2024 to 31 March 2025)

Notes

Census and police inputs enter at service_area as aggregated L2 counts (exact nesting), never apportioned.
