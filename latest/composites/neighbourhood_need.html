Domains and indicators

Economic hardship

  • Income deprivation affecting children (IDACI) (IoD 2025 (most inputs 2022/23))

Health disability

  • Children receiving Disability Living Allowance (February 2025)
  • Young people receiving PIP (March 2025)

Family housing

  • Overcrowded households with children (Census Day, 21 March 2021)
  • Lone-parent households (Census Day, 21 March 2021)
  • Young carers (Census Day, 21 March 2021)

Safety

  • Violence and sexual offences (1 April 2024 to 31 March 2025)

Notes

Overlaps with IMD by design (IDACI is benefit-based). Report alongside IMD, not instead of it (12.7).
